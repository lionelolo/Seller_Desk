SellerDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SellerDesk ("the app") helps sellers manage their own Eldorado store from an Android phone: see orders, get an alarm when a new order arrives, and manage listings. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Who we are

SellerDesk is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Eldorado.gg. Contact: lioneloloansiagian@gmail.com

What the app handles

  • Seller API credentials (Client ID and Client secret) that you type in to connect your store. They are stored encrypted on your phone and are sent only to Eldorado (eldorado.gg) to sign you in.
  • Store data returned by Eldorado on your request: orders, buyer usernames, order messages and reviews, listings, and notifications. This data is shown on your screen. It is held on your phone only while the app needs it.

What we do not do

  • We do not run any server that receives your data. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

Where your data goes

The app talks directly to Eldorado's servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Eldorado handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: to reach Eldorado.
  • Notifications, full-screen alert, vibration, wake lock: to alert you loudly when a new order or buyer message arrives, including when the phone is locked.
  • Foreground service and start at boot: to keep checking for new orders in the background and to resume after a restart.

Deleting your data

Choose "Log out and forget credentials" in Settings to erase the saved credentials and local data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. To delete your Eldorado account data, contact Eldorado.

Children

The app is intended for adults who run an online store. It is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
